GEPCO Safety Leadership Directives

Official safety messages from the highest levels of leadership

“

Safety is Not a Checklist. It is Our Culture.

To the dedicated team of GEPCO,

Every single day, you step into the field to keep the lights on for millions. You climb poles, maintain grids, and face high-voltage challenges. But let me be absolutely clear: No power supply is worth a human life.

Safety is not a set of rules we follow to appease auditors; it is the heartbeat of our operations. It is the promise you make to your family when you leave home in the morning, and the promise we make to you as an organization.

I urge you to empower yourselves. Stop unsafe work. Speak up when you see a hazard. Look out for your colleague standing next to you. Let us build a culture where safety is instinctive, not just instructional. Your life is the current that powers our nation. Go home safe, every single day.

Chief Executive Officer GEPCO

“

The Moral Compass of Our Organization.

Esteemed colleagues and stakeholders,

As the Chairman of the Board, my foremost commitment is to the preservation of our greatest asset—our people. A robust safety framework is not merely a regulatory requirement; it is the moral compass of our organization.

We must strive for a zero-harm environment, where every employee feels empowered to prioritize their well-being above all else. Safety is the foundation upon which GEPCO's sustainable future is built. It is not just about preventing accidents; it is about fostering a workplace where dignity and life are valued above all metrics.

I call upon every member of this organization to embrace safety as a personal and collective responsibility. Together, let us set a standard of excellence that protects not only our workforce but also the communities we serve. Safety is the ultimate investment.

Chairman Board of Directors GEPCO
